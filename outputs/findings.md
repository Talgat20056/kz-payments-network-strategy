# Findings — Kazakhstan payments: where should an international network invest 2027–2029?

**Answer first.** Stop treating Kaspi's closed loop as a melting iceberg. Its share is very large but **no longer growing**, so the domestic card base is stable. Put incremental money into **(1) cross-border card-not-present**, which is growing about 4x faster than domestic, and **(2) getting the dormant, cash-heavy international card base to spend**. Do not fund a head-on fight in **credit** or **QR**: the closed loop owns credit (92% of credit cards), and QR is the instrument Kaspi uses to cut out interchange.

Labels: **[data]** = as published; **[calc]** = arithmetic on published data (formula shown); **[estimate]** = depends on a stated assumption. Every number is regenerated by `src/analysis/*.py` into `outputs/metrics.csv`.

## H1 — Activation & usage → **Partially confirmed**
*The installed international card base is under-used (dormant and cash-only). The gap is real and is not closing on its own, but its sized value [estimate] does not prove it is the single biggest lever.*

| Test | Result | Threshold | Pass? |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1a Dormant share of international cards | **56.8%** (32.3m of 56.9m) [calc] | ≥ 40% | ✅ |
| H1b Change in international activation over 24m | **−0.3pp** (43.5% → 43.2%) [calc] | ≤ +1pp = not self-correcting | ✅ |
| H1c Activation gap, local − international | **18.1pp** (61.3% vs 43.2%) [calc] | ≥ 10pp | ✅ |
| H1d Cash share of card value, international vs local | **26.8% vs 5.3% = 5.0x** [calc] | ≥ 2x | ✅ |
| H1e Value of fixing it [estimate] | **+6.6% base** (2.6% low / 16.7% high) of intl cashless value | ≥ 10% confirm, < 5% kill | ◐ |

**The 2–3 numbers that decide it**
1. **32.3m dormant international cards, 56.8% of the international stock.** International cards in circulation are 56,919.9k and used cards are 24,590.7k. Dormant = circulation − used [calc]. (NBK-277, Table 1, as of 2026-10-01.) Over the same 24 months international activation moved −0.3pp (43.5% → 43.2%) [calc] (NBK-265 / NBK-277, Table 1).
2. **Mastercard is issuing without activating.** MC cards in circulation rose **+15.9%** while MC used cards fell **−4.5%** (2024-10-01 → 2026-10-01) [calc]. MC debit activation is **21.5%** (3,023.5k used ÷ 14,049.9k in circulation) [calc] (NBK-277, Table 1).
3. **International cards carry 68.4% of all cash-withdrawal value but only 25.0% of cashless value** (Jan–Sep 2026; intl cash ₸13.30trn of ₸19.43trn; intl cashless ₸36.28trn of ₸144.86trn) [calc] (NBK-277, Table 4).

**Sizing [estimate]** (`outputs/h1_sizing.csv`). Uplift = (Δactivation × 56.9m intl cards × α × ₸2.01m average cashless value per used intl card per year) + (conversion % × ₸18.4trn intl cash withdrawals over the 12 months to Sep 2026). As a share of ₸49.4trn intl cashless value [calc]:
- low (+2pp activation, α = 15%, 5% conversion) = **2.6%**
- base (+5pp, α = 25%, 10%) = **6.6%**
- high (+10pp, α = 40%, 20%) = **16.7%**

α (how much a newly activated card spends relative to today's average active card) is an assumption, not data.

Regional targeting: the top-quartile cash regions (North Kazakhstan, Kostanay, East Kazakhstan, Pavlodar, Abai) take **19.9–25.5%** of card value in cash, against **8.7%** in Almaty city [calc] (NBK-277, Table 5, Jan–Sep 2026).

**So what for a network.** The cheapest volume is already in people's wallets. Fund issuer programmes, with non-Kaspi issuers, that are paid on *active* cards and *cashless share*, not on cards issued. Target the north and east first. Stop paying issuance-volume incentives: the Mastercard pattern above shows they buy dormant plastic.

## H2 — Defensible niches → **Partially confirmed** (cross-border ✅, credit ❌, domestic e-com not testable)

| Test | Result | Threshold | Pass? |
|---|---|---|---|
| H2a Outbound growth ÷ domestic growth | **2.2x FY2025** (+28.0% vs +13.0%); **2.3x Jan–Sep 2026** (+13.3% vs +5.8%) [calc] | ≥ 1.5x | ✅ |
| H2b Outbound CNP is the largest and fastest channel | **61.3% of outbound**; **+25.6%** vs POS abroad **−1.0%** (Jan–Sep 2026) [calc] | largest and fastest | ✅ |
| H2c Inbound foreign-card value in KZ | **₸2.53trn FY2025, +0.6%; −5.1% Jan–Sep 2026** [calc] | sizing | flat |
| H2d International share of credit cards | **7.8%** (1.01m of 13.01m) [calc] | ≥ 25% confirm / < 10% kill | ❌ |
| H2e Domestic e-commerce separable? | No. "Internet & mobile" mixes purchases and P2P (79.0% of in-KZ cashless value) | — | data gap |

**The 2–3 numbers that decide it**
1. **Outbound card-not-present: ₸2.31trn in Jan–Sep 2026, +25.6% YoY** (₸1.84trn in Jan–Sep 2025) (NBK-277, Table 7) [data; growth calc]. FY2025: ₸2.58trn, +32.5% (NBK-268, Table 7). Domestic KZ-card cashless value grew **+5.8%** over the same 9 months [calc: T4 − T7].
2. **Credit belongs to the local system.** There are **13,014.5k** credit cards in circulation (NBK-277, Table 2, as of 2026-10-01). Visa + Mastercard + UnionPay credit = 618.1k + 392.6k + ~0k (Table 1), so international credit = **7.8%** and local credit ≈ 12.0m [calc]. The local system does not split by card type in Table 1, so local credit is a residual.
3. **Inbound is flat and contested.** Foreign cards in KZ = T6 total − (T4 total − T7 total) = **₸2.53trn FY2025 vs ₸2.51trn FY2024** [calc] (NBK-268 / NBK-256). Kaspi says Kaspi QR is "integrated … internationally with AliPay+" (K25, PDF p53). The residual is ±₸0.2trn sensitive to revisions, so treat it as indicative.

**So what for a network.** Outbound CNP is the niche the closed loop structurally cannot serve: KZ consumers buying from foreign merchants need a card credential. Invest in tokenisation, issuer 3-DS / authorisation-rate programmes, and cross-border e-commerce merchant partnerships. Do **not** build a credit play. Treat inbound as a defend-and-measure battle against Alipay+ via Kaspi QR, not a growth engine.

## H3 — Domestic erosion by the closed loop → **Killed as stated** (level confirmed, "growing" not)

| Test | Result | Threshold | Pass? |
|---|---|---|---|
| H3a Local-system share of KZ-card domestic cashless value | **77.0%** Jan–Sep 2026 [calc] | ≥ 60% level | ✅ level |
| H3b Trend of that share | **−1.8pp** FY24→FY25 (79.5%→77.8%); **−1.4pp** YoY Jan–Sep (78.3%→77.0%) [calc] | rising ≥ 1pp/yr | ❌ |
| H3c QR share of in-store (POS + QR) value | 50.7% FY24 → 51.8% FY25 → **51.9%** Jan–Sep 2026 (52.0% Jan–Sep 2025) [calc] | rising ≥ 1pp/yr | ❌ (plateau) |
| H3d Kaspi vs market | Kaspi QR+card TPV [calc] ÷ NBK POS+QR: **83% → 82%** (FY24→FY25); Kaspi TPV ÷ NBK cashless 22% → 23% | consistent with H3a | ✅ level |
| H3e In-store bypass share [estimate] | **66% base** (49% low / 76% high), FY2025 | base ≥ 60% | ✅ level |
| H3f Kaspi's stated intent | "continuing adoption of proprietary QR transactions, which eliminates interchange fees paid to third-party payment solutions providers" (K25 p105; also FY2024 p105 and FY2023 p109) | statement exists | ✅ |

**The 2–3 numbers that decide it**
1. **Local share of domestic card value fell 78.3% → 77.0%** (Jan–Sep 2025 → Jan–Sep 2026) [calc]. Local cashless grew **+4.0%** while international domestic cashless grew **+12.4%** [calc]. Formula: T4 local ÷ (T4 total − T7 total), assuming local-system cards are not used abroad (NBK-277, Tables 4 and 7).
2. **QR's share of in-store value has stalled at ~52%.** QR grew +10.7% and POS +11.1% (Jan–Sep 2026 YoY; QR ₸15.47trn, POS ₸14.36trn) [calc] (NBK-277, Table 6). In FY2025 QR still grew faster (+19.1% vs +14.3%) (NBK-268 / NBK-256).
3. **In-store bypass [estimate]: B = (q·QR + s·POS) ÷ (QR + POS).** Base q = 1.0, s = 0.30 gives **66%** of FY2025 in-store value outside international rails. Low (q = 0.85, s = 0.10) gives 49%; high (q = 1.0, s = 0.50) gives 76%. QR ₸19.22trn and POS ₸17.91trn come from NBK-268, Table 6. *The trend in this estimate is flat partly by construction (s is held constant), so the trend evidence is items 1–2, not this.*

Cross-check: Kaspi QR+card TPV = 69% × ₸44,219bn = **₸30.5trn** (K25 p92) [calc]. Against NBK POS+QR of ₸37.1trn that is 82% [calc]. The scopes differ: Kaspi's figure includes online card payments, and NBK's includes foreign cards. Kaspi's QR+card volume grew **+15.4%** in FY2025, slightly below NBK in-store **+16.7%** [calc]. Kaspi's total TPV did grow faster than NBK cashless (+18.8% vs +13.3%), but that gap is driven by B2B (+45%) and bills, which are not card-network battlegrounds (K25 p92).

**So what for a network.** Domestic card volume is not being hollowed out any further. International cards' domestic cashless value is growing double-digit, so defend and monetise it rather than write it off. A head-on QR war is unwinnable: QR is Kaspi's explicit interchange-avoidance tool. The domestic play is **acceptance quality at POS** (tap-to-phone / soft-POS with non-Kaspi acquirers; POS ticket ₸6,487 vs QR ₸5,068, FY2025 [calc], NBK-268 Table 6) plus **interoperability positioning with the regulator**.
