# Storyline (ghost deck) — Kazakhstan 2027–2029: where an international card network should invest

Pyramid: **Answer (slide 1)** → three supporting arguments, each a block of slides:
- **A. The closed loop is big but no longer advancing**, so defend the domestic core rather than fight a QR war (slides 2–5).
- **B. The cheapest volume is already in wallets**: dormant and cash-heavy international cards (slides 6–7).
- **C. Cross-border CNP is the structural niche; credit and inbound are not** (slides 8–9).

These lead into the **recommendations (slide 10)**.

> **Disclaimer.** Independent portfolio analysis using only public data. Not affiliated with, commissioned or endorsed by Visa, Mastercard, Kaspi.kz or the National Bank of Kazakhstan. Not investment advice. (Shown on slide 1.)

Source keys (full URL · table · as-of) are listed in `outputs/findings.md`. NBK-277 = release as of 2026-10-01; NBK-268 = as of 2026-01-01 (FY2024–25); K25 = Kaspi 20-F FY2025. [calc] = derived; [estimate] = assumption-dependent.

### Slide 1 — Executive summary
**Title:** Fund cross-border CNP and card activation; don't fight Kaspi on QR or credit
**Chart:** — (text slide)
**Source:** National Bank of Kazakhstan (NBK) payment-card release v277, as of 2026-10-01, Tables 1, 2, 4, 6, 7 (nationalbank.kz/en/news/elektronnye-bankovskie-uslugi/20544); Kaspi.kz Form 20-F FY2025, PDF p105 (ir.kaspi.kz/media/Form_20-F_2025.pdf). [calc] derived.
- **The closed loop has stopped gaining.** Kaspi's local system holds 77.0% of domestic card value, down 1.4pp YoY. QR's in-store share is flat at ~52% (NBK-277, T4/T6/T7, Jan–Sep 2026) [calc].
- **The growth is cross-border and in idle wallets.** Outbound CNP is +25.6% vs domestic +5.8% (NBK-277, T7, T4−T7) [calc]. 32.3m international cards (56.8%) are dormant, and international cards carry 68% of cash withdrawals (NBK-277, T1/T4) [calc].
- **Stay out of credit and QR.** International schemes have 7.8% of credit cards (NBK-277, T1/T2) [calc]. Kaspi says its proprietary QR "eliminates interchange fees" (K25 p105).

### Slide 3 — Closed-loop share (argument A)
**Title:** Kaspi's local system holds 77% of domestic card value, but share is slipping

### Slide 4 — QR vs POS (argument A)
**Title:** QR overtook POS in-store, but its share has stalled near 52%
**Chart:** `outputs/charts/11_qr_vs_pos.png`

### Slide 5 — Size of the bypass (argument A)
**Title:** An estimated two-thirds of in-store value bypasses networks — large but stable
**Chart:** `outputs/charts/12_bypass_estimate.png`
**Source:** NBK release v268, Table 6, FY2025 (nationalbank.kz/en/news/elektronnye-bankovskie-uslugi/18537); Kaspi.kz Form 20-F FY2025 PDF p92, p105; FY2024 p105; FY2023 p109 (ir.kaspi.kz/financial-information). Bypass share is an [estimate]: assumptions on slide.
- [estimate] B = (q·QR + s·POS) ÷ (QR + POS), FY2025: base 66% (q = 1.0, s = 0.30), low 49% (0.85, 0.10), high 76% (1.0, 0.50). Inputs QR ₸19.22trn and POS ₸17.91trn (NBK-268, T6).
- Kaspi QR+card TPV = 69% × ₸44,219bn = ₸30.5trn (K25 p92) [calc], which is 82% of NBK POS+QR. FY2025 growth: Kaspi QR+card +15.4% vs NBK in-store +16.7% [calc].
- Kaspi states its intent: proprietary QR "eliminates interchange fees paid to third-party payment solutions providers" (K25 p105; FY2024 p105; FY2023 p109).

### Slide 10 — Recommendations
**Title:** Three moves for 2027–29: cross-border CNP, activation, in-store acceptance
**Chart:** — (Impact × Effort table)
**Source:** KPIs are trackable monthly from NBK payment-card releases (nationalbank.kz/en/news/elektronnye-bankovskie-uslugi/). Impact sizing: outputs/h1_sizing.csv [estimate]; targets are judgement [target].

| # | Move | Impact | Effort | KPI that proves it worked (public, NBK) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Win outbound cross-border CNP.** Tokenisation and issuer authorisation-rate programmes with KZ issuers; partnerships with the cross-border e-commerce merchants KZ consumers use; FX-transparent pricing. | **High.** Fastest-growing pool (+25.6%), and closed-loop QR cannot serve foreign merchants. | **Low–Med.** Uses existing network products. | KZ-card internet & mobile value abroad (NBK T7) keeps growing ≥ 25%/yr through 2029 [target] |
| 2 | **Pay-for-activation and cash-to-card with non-Kaspi issuers.** Incentives tied to *active* cards and cashless share (not issuance), north and east first. | **Med–High.** +6.6% intl cashless value base case [estimate] (range 2.6–16.7%). | **Medium.** Issuer co-investment and data sharing. | International activation ratio (NBK T1) 43% → ≥ 48% by end-2029; intl-card cash share of value (NBK T4) 27% → ≤ 20% [target] |
| 3 | **Defend in-store card acceptance.** Tap-to-phone / soft-POS with non-Kaspi acquirers in larger-ticket categories; engage NBK on interoperable acceptance. | **Medium.** Protects the ~1/3–1/2 of in-store value still on cards [estimate]. | **High.** Acquirer economics plus regulator engagement. | POS share of in-store (POS+QR) value (NBK T6) ≥ 48% (QR ≤ 52%); intl share of domestic card value (NBK T4−T7) +≥1pp/yr [target] |

- Explicitly **not** recommended: a network-branded credit push (7.8% share) and a QR price war, because QR is Kaspi's interchange-avoidance tool.
- Targets are judgement-based thresholds [target], set so they can be checked each month from NBK releases.
